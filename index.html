Arina's Romance Bookshelf

I read romance. This genre had me in a chokehold a long time ago, but I started reading more and more two years ago. Last year alone I read almost 20 books, all while managing schoolwork. I chose each book carefully and ended up reading it almost non-stop. Romance is just the one.

What I like about it

The promise. You know going in that the two people will end up together, and that frees the book up to spend its time on how they get there instead of on whether they will. A good romance is a book about two people slowly running out of excuses.

Where I'm headed next

Romantasy, mostly. Dragons, royal courts, betrayal, and tension that only exists in another imaginary universe.
