Favorites

The ones I'd hand to somebody who says they don't read romance.

Tropes I will always fall for

  • Enemies to lovers — the slow, grudging version
  • Forbidden Relationships (age gap, against the world, etc.)
  • Billionaire romance.
  • Mature MMC and FMC where they know they can lean on each other.

Tropes I skip

  • Big misunderstanding that a single conversation would fix.
  • @buse disguised as love and care (mostly met in dark romance).
  • Second chance but one of them cheated (and cheating in general).
