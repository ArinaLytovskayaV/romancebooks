# About this site

A small hand-typed site about romance novels — my actual hobby.

- `index.html` — the home page. Says who I am and why I read the genre. Links to the favorites page and pulls in the bookshelf photo.
- `favorites.html` — the second page. Lists the tropes I love and the ones I skip. Links back home.
- `style.css` — the one stylesheet, linked from both pages. Sets the font, the column width, the colors, and the spacing.
- `images/bookshelf.jpg` — a photo of my own shelf, referenced by `index.html`.
- `ABOUT.md` — this file. Explains what each file is for.
